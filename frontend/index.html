How LandScope Works

Multispectral Band Mathematics & Sentinel-2 Pipeline

1. NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index)

Measures live green vegetation canopy. Healthy chlorophyll strongly absorbs visible Red light (Band 4, 665nm) and reflects Near-Infrared (Band 8, 842nm).

NDVI = (NIR - RED) / (NIR + RED)  [Threshold > 0.3]
2. NDBI (Normalized Difference Built-Up Index)

Highlights impervious built structures, asphalt, concrete, and urban development. Concrete reflects Short-Wave Infrared (Band 11, 1610nm) more intensely than NIR.

NDBI = (SWIR - NIR) / (SWIR + NIR)  [Threshold > 0.1]
3. Data Source: ESA Sentinel-2 MSI

High-resolution (10m - 20m) Top-of-Atmosphere / Surface Reflectance imagery queried directly through the Google Earth Engine Cloud API, available from 2015 to present.
